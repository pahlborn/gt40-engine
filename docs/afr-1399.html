165cc SBF Street Cylinder Head

AFR #1399 (58cc, 1/2" Head Bolts, Stud Mount)
MSRP $2,126.00 (Pair)

Overview

AFR's 165cc "Street" cylinder heads are the preferred choice over re-worked factory heads. They feature 100% CNC ported intake, exhaust & chambers, A356 aluminum castings and high quality components throughout. These are the only AFR heads compatible with the OEM piston valve relief. Recommended for street, towing, or street/strip engines with displacements up to 351 cubic inches, operating up to 6000 RPM.

Legal for 1995 and older emissions controlled vehicles under CARB EO #D-250-3.
