4a – Before Start (Vor dem Start)
1. Pre-Oil (Vorölen) until oil at all 16 rockers

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Priming tool (Melling PT11 or long hex bit in drill)
  • Drill (slow speed, clockwise rotation!)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Remove distributor (access to oil pump drive)
  • 2. Insert priming tool, spin with drill
  • 3. Watch oil pressure gauge – must show pressure
  • 4. Rotate crankshaft 4× 90° during priming (supply all bearings)
  • 5. Remove valve covers – oil must appear at all 16 rockers
WARNING (WARNUNG): No oil pressure after 30 sec = STOP! Investigate (shaft not engaged, pump empty, plug missing). – Kein Öldruck nach 30 Sek. = STOPP! Ursache suchen.

Practical Tip (Praxis-Tipp)

  • IMMER vorölen vor dem Erststart! Melling PT11 + Bohrmaschine, bis Öl an allen Rockern austritt. (ALWAYS pre-oil before first start! Melling PT11 + drill until oil appears at all rockers.)
2. Rotate Crank 4× 90° during pre-oiling (Kurbelwelle drehen beim Vorölen)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Pre-oiling in progress (priming tool spinning in drill)
  • Wrench or socket on harmonic balancer bolt for crank rotation
  • Oil pressure gauge connected and showing pressure

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Large wrench or breaker bar on damper bolt (15/16" or 24 mm)
  • Priming tool (Melling PT11) still engaged in drill
  • Helper recommended: one person runs drill, other rotates crank

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Start priming: With priming tool spinning in drill, allow oil pressure to build and stabilize (Vorölen starten, Druck aufbauen lassen)
  2. First 90° rotation: While drill is running, slowly rotate crankshaft 90° clockwise with wrench on damper bolt (Kurbel 90° im Uhrzeigersinn drehen)
  3. Pause 15–20 seconds: Hold position and let oil flow reach the newly-positioned bearings (15–20 Sekunden warten)
  4. Second 90° rotation: Rotate another 90° (total 180°) – pause again (weitere 90° drehen)
  5. Third 90° rotation: Rotate another 90° (total 270°) – pause (weitere 90°)
  6. Fourth 90° rotation: Rotate final 90° (full 360° revolution) – all bearings have now been in every position relative to oil feed holes (letzte 90° – alle Lager waren in jeder Position)
  7. Watch for oil at rockers: Continue priming and rotating until oil appears at all 16 rocker arms (bis Öl an allen 16 Kipphebeln austritt)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Rotating the crank ensures ALL main bearings, rod bearings, and cam bearings receive oil – oil feed holes in bearings only align at certain crank positions
  • Rotate SLOWLY – the priming tool cannot supply oil as fast as a running engine
  • If oil pressure drops during rotation: this is normal – new bearings are being loaded with oil
  • Ideal to do 2–3 full revolutions total for thorough pre-oiling
  • Check under engine for any oil leaks while priming (auf Leckagen prüfen)
3. Oil Pressure during Pre-Oil (Öldruck beim Vorölen)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Mechanical oil pressure gauge connected (do NOT rely on electric sender for pre-oil)
  • Priming tool engaged and drill running
  • Oil level verified on dipstick before priming

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Minimum during priming≥ 15 psi (1.0 bar)
Good reading25–40 psi (1.7–2.8 bar)
Time to pressure< 30 seconds

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Start drill: Run priming tool at moderate speed (not full RPM) and watch gauge immediately (Bohrmaschine starten, Manometer beobachten)
  2. Pressure within 30 seconds: Gauge should show 15+ psi within 30 seconds of priming. If YES: continue priming with crank rotation (Druck innerhalb 30 Sek.)
  3. No pressure after 30 seconds: STOP! Investigate immediately: primer shaft not engaging pump, oil pump not primed (empty), pickup tube seal leaking, or wrong shaft length (STOPP falls kein Druck!)
  4. Continue until oil at rockers: Prime until oil is visible flowing at all 16 rocker arm pivot points – remove valve covers to verify (bis Öl an allen Kipphebeln sichtbar)
  5. Record pressure: Note the stabilized pressure reading on gauge for reference (Öldruck notieren)
No oil pressure after 30 seconds = STOP IMMEDIATELY! Common causes: (1) Primer shaft too short – not engaging pump rotor, (2) Oil pump was not pre-filled with assembly lube – cannot self-prime, (3) Pickup tube O-ring leaking air, (4) Oil level too low. (Kein Druck nach 30 Sek. = SOFORT stoppen und Ursache suchen!)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Melling M-68 high-volume pump should show strong pressure during priming if properly filled with assembly lube before installation
  • Drill speed matters: too fast can aerate oil, too slow won’t build pressure. Moderate speed is best
  • If oil never reaches one bank of rockers: suspect plugged oil gallery on that side
  • Total priming time: typically 3–5 minutes with crank rotation to fully oil all bearings and push oil to rockers
  • After priming, reinstall valve covers and distributor before starting
4. Fill Break-In Oil (ZDDP!) – Öl einfüllen

Fill Quantity (Füllmenge)

Oil quantity (depends on pan)5–7 quarts (4.7–6.6 L)
Viscosity (Viskosität)10W-30 or 15W-40

Recommended Products (Empfohlene Produkte)

  • Brad Penn 10W-30 (high ZDDP content)
  • Driven BR30 Break-In Oil
  • Lucas Hot Rod & Classic

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • ZDDP (zinc dialkyldithiophosphate) protects cam lobes & lifters during break-in
  • Modern oil (API SN+) has reduced ZDDP – not suitable for break-in
5. Fill & Bleed Cooling System (Kühlsystem füllen & entlüften)

Fill Quantity (Füllmenge)

SBF 302 Cooling System~14 quarts (13.2 L) total
Coolant Mix (Kühlmittel)50/50 antifreeze/distilled water
Thermostat Opens180°F (82°C) – 192°F (89°C)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Funnel with long neck (Trichter)
  • Drain pan (Auffangwanne)
  • Bleed valve wrench (if equipped)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Verify all drain petcocks closed (alle Ablass-Hahne geschlossen)
  • 2. Remove heater hose at highest point for air bleed (Heizungsschlauch am höchsten Punkt öffnen)
  • 3. Fill slowly through radiator cap or filler neck (langsam einfüllen)
  • 4. Watch for coolant at heater hose bleed point – close when flowing (schließen wenn Kühlmittel kommt)
  • 5. Fill expansion/overflow tank to COLD mark (Ausgleichsbehälter bis KALT-Markierung)
  • 6. After first warm-up: recheck level with engine cold (nach Warmlauf: Pegel kalt nachprüfen)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Mid-engine cars: fill system with engine tilted if possible – air pockets at highest point in hoses
  • Run engine to operating temp with radiator cap off (carefully!) to self-bleed
  • Squeeze upper radiator hose to push trapped air through system
  • Recheck level daily for first week – air pockets work out slowly
6. Check Fuel System (Kraftstoffsystem prüfen)

Fuel Pressure (Kraftstoffdruck)

Mechanical pump (mechanische Pumpe)5.5–7.0 psi (0.38–0.48 bar)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Too high = carburetor floods (Vergaser läuft über) – may need pressure regulator
  • Check all connections for leaks before start!
7. Static Ignition Timing ~12° BTDC (Zündzeitpunkt statisch einstellen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Crank turning socket or breaker bar on balancer bolt
  • 1/2" wrench for distributor hold-down clamp
  • Test light or multimeter (for points/trigger verification)

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Initial Static Timing10–12° BTDC
TDC Mark0° mark on balancer aligned with pointer

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Rotate crank to TDC #1 compression stroke – both valves closed, TDC mark aligned (Kurbelwelle auf OT #1 Kompressionstakt drehen)
  • 2. Verify rotor points toward #1 terminal on cap (Verteilerfinger zeigt auf Zyl. 1 Anschluss)
  • 3. Rotate crank BACKWARDS to 10–12° BTDC mark on balancer
  • 4. Loosen distributor hold-down, rotate body until trigger/pickup just activates
  • 5. Tighten hold-down clamp – snug, not final (engine will need timing light adjustment at warm idle)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • 10–12° is a safe starting point – adjust with timing light after engine is running
  • Verify firing order: 1-5-4-2-6-3-7-8 (Ford 302) – wires must be in correct cap terminals
  • MSD magnetic pickup: no test light needed – just align rotor to #1 at static timing position
8. MSD 6AL Rev Limiter: 6,500 RPM (Drehzahlbegrenzer)

RPM Module Selection (Modul-Auswahl)

Recommended Chip6,200–6,500 RPM
Module TypeMSD RPM Module (2-pin plug)
XE274HR Max RPM~6,500 RPM (per COMP cam card)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Locate MSD 6AL box – find RPM module plug (2-pin connector)
  • 2. Select appropriate RPM pill/chip: 6,200 RPM (conservative) or 6,500 RPM (matches cam)
  • 3. Plug module firmly into connector – ensure positive click
  • 4. If no module installed: MSD defaults to ~9,000 RPM (no protection!)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • For break-in: use 6,200 RPM chip – swap to 6,500 after break-in complete
  • Carry spare module chips of different RPM values in toolbox
  • MSD 6AL also has 2-step launch control – optional for track use
9. Recheck Preload all 16 valves (Preload nochmals prüfen)

Target Value (Sollwert)

Preload Morel 5327 HLT1/2 turn past zero lash (1/2 Umdrehung nach Nullspiel)
Engine TemperatureCold (Kalt)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Rotate crank so valve’s lifter is on base circle (Nocken-Grundkreis)
  • 2. Loosen polylock set screw, back off adjusting nut slightly
  • 3. Spin pushrod while re-tightening adjusting nut
  • 4. Zero lash = pushrod just stops spinning freely
  • 5. From zero lash: 1/2 turn further = preload
  • 6. Lock polylock set screw – recheck that nut did not move
  • 7. Repeat for ALL 16 valves using EO/IC method

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Parts may have shifted since Phase 3 assembly – always recheck before first start
  • Use EO/IC method: when exhaust opens → set intake; when intake closes → set exhaust
  • If any pushrod feels loose or doesn’t want to spin – investigate before starting!

Wiring Checklist

  • ☐ MSD 6AL box: heavy red wire to battery + (12V fused)
  • ☐ MSD 6AL box: heavy black wire to solid engine/chassis ground
  • ☐ MSD white wire to ignition switch (12V key-on)
  • ☐ MSD coil wires: orange + dark green to MSD Blaster coil
  • ☐ MSD magnetic pickup: violet & green wires from distributor
  • ☐ Tach signal: gray wire from MSD to tachometer
  • ☐ Spark plug wires: correct firing order on distributor cap
  • ☐ Starter solenoid: battery cable, ignition trigger wire
  • ☐ Alternator: charge wire + field wire
  • ☐ Oil pressure sender: wire to gauge
  • ☐ Water temperature sender: wire to gauge

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Trace each spark plug wire from cap to plug – verify firing order physically, not from memory
  • MSD 6AL requires dedicated 12V hot (not shared with other accessories) – use 10 AWG wire
  • Ground strap from engine block to chassis is mandatory – poor ground = no start or weak spark

  • ☐ Fire extinguisher (ABC rated, min. 5 lbs) within arm’s reach (Feuerlöscher griffbereit)
  • ☐ Helper present – one person at the controls, one watching for leaks/fire (Helfer anwesend)
  • ☐ Exhaust ventilation – garage door open or exhaust extraction in place (Abgasabsaugung oder offenes Tor)
  • ☐ No fuel leaks – check all fittings, fuel line connections, carburetor base (keine Kraftstoffleckagen)
  • ☐ Oil level correct on dipstick (Ölstand korrekt auf Peilstab)
  • ☐ Coolant level correct (Kühlmittelstand korrekt)
  • ☐ All bolts torqued – visual inspection of all external fasteners (alle Schrauben angezogen)
  • ☐ Battery fully charged – 12.6V minimum (Batterie vollgeladen)
  • ☐ No loose wires near exhaust or moving parts (keine losen Kabel)
  • ☐ Throttle returns to idle when released (Gas geht in Leerlauf zurück)
  • ☐ Kill switch accessible – know how to shut off ignition immediately (Notausschalter erreichbar)
4b – First Start (Erststart)
12. START – immediately 2,000–2,500 RPM! (Sofort auf 2.000–2.500 U/min!)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • ALL pre-start checklist items completed (Steps 1–11)
  • Helper present with fire extinguisher
  • Exhaust ventilation in place (garage door open)
  • Throttle linkage verified – can hold 2,000–2,500 RPM
  • Timing set to ~12° BTDC (static)
  • Tachometer connected and visible
  • Oil pressure gauge and temperature gauge visible from driver position

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Tachometer (via MSD 6AL gray tach wire)
  • Oil pressure gauge (mechanical preferred)
  • Coolant temperature gauge
  • Timing light (for adjustment after startup)
  • Wrench for distributor hold-down (timing adjustment)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Prime carburetor: Pump throttle 2–3 times to squirt accelerator pump fuel into intake (Vergaser pumpen)
  2. Crank engine: Turn key / hit starter – engine should fire within 5–10 seconds if timing and fuel are correct (Motor starten)
  3. IMMEDIATELY go to 2,000–2,500 RPM: As soon as engine fires, bring RPM up to 2,000–2,500 and HOLD IT THERE. Do NOT let it idle! (SOFORT auf 2.000–2.500 U/min – NICHT im Leerlauf lassen!)
  4. Check oil pressure FIRST: Within the first 5 seconds, confirm oil pressure on gauge. If NO pressure: shut down immediately! (Sofort Öldruck prüfen – kein Druck = SOFORT abstellen!)
  5. Hold 2,000–2,500 RPM for 20–30 minutes: This is the cam/distributor gear break-in period. Vary RPM between 2,000–3,000 every few minutes (20–30 Min. halten, Drehzahl variieren)
  6. Monitor continuously: Watch oil pressure, coolant temp, and listen for unusual sounds throughout the break-in period (durchgehend überwachen)
  7. Check for leaks: Have helper visually inspect for oil, coolant, and fuel leaks during the run (Helfer prüft auf Leckagen)
  8. Adjust timing if needed: With timing light on #1, verify and adjust initial timing while engine is running (Zündung mit Stroboskop prüfen)
CRITICAL: Do NOT idle a fresh engine! At idle, oil splash from the crank is insufficient to lubricate cam lobes, lifter rollers, and distributor gear. The 2,000+ RPM ensures adequate oil circulation and splash lubrication to all components. Even hydraulic roller cams need elevated RPM for distributor gear break-in. (NICHT im Leerlauf laufen lassen – bei niedriger Drehzahl ist die Ölversorgung unzureichend!)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • If engine won’t start within 15 seconds of cranking: STOP. Check ignition timing, fuel delivery, firing order. Do not run starter continuously (nicht endlos orgeln)
  • Vary RPM between 2,000–3,000 RPM every 2–3 minutes – constant RPM loads the same spots repeatedly
  • Coolant temp should reach thermostat opening (~180°F / 82°C) within 10–15 minutes – this is normal
  • Minor exhaust smoke at first start is normal – assembly lube and oil residue burning off
  • Have a plan for what to do if something goes wrong: kill switch location, fire extinguisher location
  • After 20–30 minutes: allow engine to idle briefly, then shut down. Break-in is complete

Time Estimate (Zeitbedarf)

  • 20–30 min. continuous running at elevated RPM
13. Oil Pressure at 2,000 RPM warm: 40–55 psi (2.8–3.8 bar)

Target Values (Sollwerte)

At 2,000 RPM warm (180°F / 82°C)40–55 psi (2.8–3.8 bar)
Rule of thumb~10 psi per 1,000 RPM minimum
Cold startup (first seconds)60–80 psi (normal – thick oil)
Hot idle (after warm-up)25–35 psi (1.7–2.4 bar)

What to Monitor (Worauf achten)

  • Normal: Pressure drops as oil warms up – thinner oil = lower pressure (normal)
  • Low pressure (<30 psi at 2,000 RPM warm): Check bearing clearances, oil pump, pickup tube seal, oil level
  • Very low (<15 psi at any RPM): SHUT DOWN immediately – investigate before running again (SOFORT abstellen!)
  • High pressure (>60 psi warm): Oil still cold, wrong viscosity, or relief valve stuck closed

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Read at steady 2,000 RPM: With engine at operating temperature (180°F+ coolant), hold steady 2,000 RPM and read oil pressure gauge (bei Betriebstemperatur ablesen)
  2. Compare to spec: Melling M-68 high-volume pump should provide 40–55 psi at 2,000 RPM with standard bearing clearances (mit Soll vergleichen)
  3. Check at 3,000 RPM: Increase to 3,000 RPM briefly – pressure should rise proportionally to ~50–65 psi (bei 3.000 U/min prüfen)
  4. Check at idle: Drop to idle – should hold 25–35 psi when warm. Below 15 psi at idle is concerning (im Leerlauf prüfen)
  5. Record readings: Document oil pressure at 2,000 RPM warm as baseline for future comparison (Werte dokumentieren)
Below 10 psi at ANY RPM = SHUT DOWN! Low oil pressure will destroy main bearings, rod bearings, and cam bearings within minutes. Possible causes: excessive bearing clearances, oil pump failure, air leak at pickup, low oil level. (Unter 10 psi = SOFORT abstellen – Lagerschaden droht!)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Melling M-68 is a high-volume pump – expect slightly higher pressure than stock pump
  • Oil pressure is a function of clearances + pump volume + relief valve setting – not just the pump
  • 10W-30 break-in oil will show lower warm pressure than 15W-40 – both are normal for their viscosity
  • Pressure fluctuation of ±5 psi at steady RPM is normal – large swings indicate air in system or pickup uncovering
  • After break-in oil change: re-check pressure with new oil to establish true baseline
14. Visual Inspection: no leaks (Sichtkontrolle: keine Leckagen)

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte) – at idle, with helper watching

  • Oil (Öl): Valve covers, oil pan gasket, timing cover, oil filter, oil pressure sender, rear main seal, front crank seal
  • Coolant (Kühlmittel): Water pump gasket, intake manifold end seals, heater hose connections, radiator hoses, thermostat housing
  • Fuel (Kraftstoff): Carburetor base, fuel line fittings, fuel pump connections, fuel filter
  • Exhaust (Abgas): Header/manifold flange gaskets – listen for ticking sound = exhaust leak

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Use a flashlight and mirror to check underside and rear of engine
  • A clean engine shows leaks immediately – wipe down before starting
  • Small oil weeping at valve cover is common – re-torque to 4–6 ft-lbs after first heat cycle
  • Any fuel leak = SHUT DOWN immediately! (Kraftstoffleck = SOFORT abstellen!)

Photo Documentation (Fotodokumentation)

  • Photo of clean engine before first start (reference)
  • Photo of any leak location found
15. Coolant Temperature – thermostat opens (Kühlmitteltemp. – Thermostat öffnet)

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Coolant Normal Operating180–195°F (82–91°C)
Oil Normal Operating180–220°F (82–104°C)
Thermostat Opens180°F (82°C) typical
Overheating Threshold> 230°F (110°C) = SHUT DOWN

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Thermostat opening is felt as sudden upper radiator hose getting hot (Thermostat öffnet = oberer Kühlerschlauch wird plötzlich heiß)
  • If temp keeps climbing past 210°F: verify fan operation, check for air pockets in cooling system
  • Oil temp lags coolant by several minutes – normal
  • Watch for temp spike = head gasket issue, air in cooling system, or electric fan not activating
If temperature exceeds 230°F (110°C) – SHUT DOWN immediately! Overheating a fresh engine with fresh assembly lube can damage cam lobes, bearings, and head gasket. (Bei über 230°F SOFORT abstellen!)
16. Check Timing Warm (Zündung warm prüfen – Stroboskop)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Timing light (NO digital/dial-back with MSD! – KEIN digitales Rückstell-Stroboskop mit MSD!)
  • Wrench for distributor clamp (Verteiler-Klemmschraube)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Engine at operating temperature (Betriebstemperatur)
  • 2. Disconnect vacuum advance hose, plug port (Unterdruckschlauch ab, Anschluss verschließen)
  • 3. Timing light on #1 spark plug wire (Stroboskop an Zündkabel Zyl. 1)
  • 4. Point at timing marks on harmonic balancer / timing cover (Markierungen am Dämpfer / Steuerdeckel)
  • 5. Loosen distributor clamp, rotate slowly to desired degree (e.g. 12° BTDC)
  • 6. Tighten clamp, recheck (timing can shift during clamping – Zündung kann sich beim Festziehen verschieben)

18. Final Timing: 34–36° total advance (Gesamt-Zündzeitpunkt)

Basics – Ignition Advance System (Grundlagen – Zündverstellung)

  • Initial Timing (Grundzündung): Set with timing light at idle, vacuum disconnected – e.g. 10–14° BTDC
  • Centrifugal Advance (Fliehkraftverstellung): Mechanical advance via weights & springs inside distributor – adds degrees as RPM rises
  • Total Timing (Gesamtzündung): Initial + Centrifugal – all-in by ~3,000–3,500 RPM. Target: 32–36° total
  • Vacuum Advance (Unterdruckverstellung): Additional advance at part-load cruise for economy & lower cylinder temps – +8–10°

19. Re-Torque Head Nuts (engine cold! / Motor kalt nachziehen!)

Prerequisites (Voraussetzungen)

  • Engine FULLY cooled (overnight best) – Motor KOMPLETT abgekühlt (am besten über Nacht)
  • First heat cycle completed

Re-Torque Values (Nachzugs-Werte)

Head Studs ARP 154-4003 Long/Blind80 ft-lbs (108 Nm)
Head Studs ARP 154-4003 Short/Water70 ft-lbs (95 Nm)
Intake Manifold Bolts18 ft-lbs (24 Nm)
LubricantARP Ultra-Torque (already applied)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Remove valve covers (Ventildeckel abnehmen)
  • 2. Re-torque head studs: cross pattern, center outward, to original spec (Kreuzschema von Mitte nach außen)
  • 3. Re-torque intake manifold bolts: cross pattern to 18 ft-lbs (24 Nm)
  • 4. Recheck all 16 rocker preloads (1/2 turn past zero lash) – head re-torque may shift preload
  • 5. Reinstall valve covers

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Copper gaskets (CP331) and aluminum heads settle during first heat cycle – re-torque is essential
  • Some studs may turn slightly further – this is normal (gasket compression)
  • If a stud turns more than 1/4 turn – something may have shifted – investigate
  • After re-torque, no further re-torques needed unless head is removed
20. Oil Change – Cut Filter Open (Filter aufschneiden)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. After 30–50 km (20–30 mi) or 1 hour runtime, drain oil warm (Öl warm ablassen)
  • 2. Cut filter open with flange cutter or band saw (Filter mit Flanschschneider oder Bandsäge aufschneiden)
  • 3. Unfold filter paper and hold against light (Filterpapier auseinanderfalten, gegen Licht halten)
  • 4. Clean paper = inspection passed (Sauberes Papier = Prüfung bestanden)
  • 5. Metal particles = find the cause, do not ignore! (Metallpartikel = Ursache finden!)

Photo Documentation (Fotodokumentation)

  • Filter paper (clean = reference for future comparisons)
  • If particles: close-up + determine material (aluminum, steel, copper)

Time Estimate (Zeitbedarf)

  • ~30 min. incl. drain & inspection
21. Read Spark Plugs (Kerzenbild lesen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Spark plug socket 5/8" / 16 mm
  • Magnifying glass (Lupe) for insulator inspection
  • Good lighting

Reading Guide (Kerzenbild-Deutung)

  • Light tan / gray (Hellbraun / Grau): IDEAL – correct mixture, good combustion (Idealer Zustand)
  • White / blistered (Weiß / Blasen): TOO LEAN – dangerous! Richen mixture or check for vacuum leaks (Zu mager – gefährlich!)
  • Black / sooty (Schwarz / rußig): TOO RICH – reduce jet size or check choke (Zu fett)
  • Black / oily / wet (Schwarz / ölig): OIL FOULING – check valve seals, rings, PCV (Verschmutzt mit Öl)
  • Aluminum deposits (Alu-Ablagerungen): DETONATION – piston damage! Retard timing, richen mixture (Klopfschäden!)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Engine at operating temperature, run for at least 10 minutes under varying load
  • 2. Shut off engine at 2,500 RPM (do NOT idle first – idle enriches the reading)
  • 3. Pull ALL 8 plugs – lay out in cylinder order (alle 8 Kerzen ziehen – in Zylinderreihenfolge auslegen)
  • 4. Compare all 8 – should be uniform color (alle 8 vergleichen – gleichmäßige Farbe)
  • 5. One cylinder different from others = that cylinder has a specific issue

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Install fresh plugs before the reading run for clearest results
  • DellOrto DRLA 45: each barrel feeds 4 cylinders – if one bank is different, adjust that carburetor
  • Photograph each plug for comparison at later tuning sessions

Photo Documentation (Fotodokumentation)

  • All 8 plugs laid out in order – reference for future readings
22. Synchronize DellOrto, Log Lambda (DellOrto synchronisieren, Lambda loggen)

Procedure – Carburetor Mounting (Vergaser-Montage)

  • 1. Carburetor base gasket dry – no sealant (Vergaser-Fußdichtung trocken – kein Dichtmittel)
  • 2. Mount evenly in cross-pattern (Gleichmäßig über Kreuz anziehen)
  • 3. Connect fuel line + throttle linkage + choke cable (Kraftstoffleitung + Gasgestänge + Choke anschließen)
  • 4. Install fuel filter between pump and carburetor (Kraftstofffilter zwischen Pumpe und Vergaser)

Practical Tip (Praxis-Tipp)

  • Nicht überziehen! Handfest + max 1/4 Umdrehung. Vergaser-Flansch ist empfindlich. (Do not overtighten! Hand-tight + max 1/4 turn. Carburetor flange is delicate.)
