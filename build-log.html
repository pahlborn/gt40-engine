Short Block on Stand (Short Block auf dem Ständer)
Block Visual Inspection (Sichtprüfung)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Lift short block from crate (2 persons, ~155 lbs / 70 kg)
  • Mount on engine stand (adapter for 302/351W pattern)
  • Remove preservation / shipping oil

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Work light / LED inspection lamp
  • Inspection mirror (dental or telescoping)
  • Magnifying glass for hairline cracks (Haarrisse)

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte)

  • Cylinder Bores (Laufflächen): No scoring, no ridge at TDC
  • Sealing Surfaces (Dichtflächen): Deck, intake rails, timing cover face – no nicks or burrs
  • Threaded Holes (Gewindebohrungen): No damaged threads, no debris in blind holes
  • Freeze Plugs (Froststopfen): All tight, no rust
  • Oil Galleries (Ölkanäle): Gallery plugs present and sealed

Photo Documentation (Fotodokumentation)

  • Deck surface both banks
  • ID plate / casting number
  • Any anomalies (casting flaws, scratches)

Time Estimate (Zeitbedarf)

  • ~30 min. incl. unpacking & mounting
Chase Threads & Blow Out Blind Holes (Gewinde durchjagen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Tap set: 7/16"-14, 3/8"-16, 5/16"-18 (11.11 mm, 9.53 mm, 7.94 mm)
  • Compressed air gun with extension
  • Safety glasses (mandatory!)
  • T-handle tap wrench

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • Brake cleaner (residual dirt)
  • Clean lint-free rags
WARNING: Blind holes must be oil- and debris-free! Fluid in a blind hole acts hydraulically – the stud can crack the block.

Release Criteria (Freigabe)

  • All holes free of chips and residue
  • Tap runs freely in every hole

Time Estimate

  • ~45 min.
Measure Deck Height (Deckhöhe messen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Depth micrometer (Tiefenmikrometer) or depth gauge
  • Straightedge (Haarlineal) + feeler gauge (Fühlerlehre)

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Deck Height (BHE)8.206" (208.43 mm)
Tolerance (Toleranz)± 0.005" (0.13 mm)
Ford 302 Nominal8.206" (208.43 mm)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Measure at least 4 locations per bank (front, rear, left, right)
  • Deck height + piston deck clearance = quench distance (determines compression & detonation margin)
Piston Deck Clearance (Kolbenstand) – all 8 cylinders

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Dial indicator (Messuhr) with magnetic base
  • Straightedge (Haarlineal) across bore
  • Crank turning tool or box wrench

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Deck Clearance (Kolbenstand)~0.010" (0.25 mm) below deck
MethodStraightedge + dial indicator

Practical Tips

  • Most critical measurement of the entire build! Piston deck clearance determines compression ratio, quench distance, and head gasket choice.
  • Measure Cyl. 1 + 5 first – the spread reveals block machining precision
  • Rotate crank to exact TDC (highest point), not slightly off
  • Measure each bore at thrust side AND anti-thrust side

Photo Documentation

  • Dial indicator on straightedge over bore (method documentation)
  • Reading at Cyl 1 and Cyl 5
Rotate Crankshaft by Hand (Kurbelwelle drehen) – no binding

Criteria

  • Crankshaft turns smoothly through full 720°
  • No sudden resistance or binding
  • No noises (scraping, clicking)

Practical Tips

  • Turn at flywheel end by hand (more leverage)
  • Binding = bearing issue or debris in oil gallery

Installed Spring Height (Federeinbauhöhe) – all 16

Tools

  • Installed-height micrometer or caliper (Messschieber)
  • Spring height gauge (steel plate with hole)

Target Values

AFR Spec Installed HeightPer AFR data sheet ± 0.015" (0.38 mm)
Correction if out of specShims under spring

Practical Tips

  • Measure without spring: install retainer + keepers, measure gap from spring seat to retainer underside
  • Spread > 0.030" (0.76 mm) between valves = rework valve seats or use shims
Coil Bind Clearance ≥ 0.060" (1.52 mm) over max. lift

Calculation (Berechnung)

  • Max. Valve Lift (exhaust): 0.565" (14.35 mm) – this IS the valve lift (at-the-valve, not lobe lift)
  • Formula: Installed Height − Max Lift − Coil Bind Height = Clearance
  • Clearance must be ≥ 0.060" (1.52 mm), otherwise spring or retainer failure
  • Coil bind height is on the spring data sheet (PAC/AFR #8605)
Open Spring Pressure at 0.565" (14.35 mm) lift

Tools

  • Spring tester (Federprüfstand) or calculate from rate

Practical Tips

  • Excessive open pressure overloads Morel 5327 link-bars
  • Morel recommendation: max. ~400 lbs open for hydraulic roller

Install Lifter on Test Cylinder #1 (Prüfzylinder)
Degree Camshaft (Nockenwelle degreen) – verify ICL

Tools

  • Degree wheel min. 9" (229 mm) diameter
  • Pointer (Zeiger), mounted to block
  • Dial indicator with lifter adapter
  • Piston stop (steel or rubber)
  • Checking spring (light spring, replaces valve spring)

Target Values – COMP XE274HR

Intake Centerline (ICL)Per cam card (laut Cam Card)
Duration @.050" Int/Exh224° / 232°
Lobe Separation Angle (LSA)112°
Valve Lift Int/Exh0.555" / 0.565" (14.10 / 14.35 mm)

Procedure

  • 1. Mount degree wheel on crank snout, pointer on block
  • 2. Find true TDC: install piston stop, rotate crank against stop (both directions), split the difference = TDC
  • 3. Place dial indicator on #1 intake lifter
  • 4. Slowly rotate cam, find max lift point
  • 5. Read degree wheel at .050" (1.27 mm) lifter rise on each side of max lift
  • 6. Midpoint = Intake Centerline
EVERY camshaft must be degreed! Manufacturing tolerances can mean 2–4° deviation. This significantly changes engine character.

Photo Documentation

  • Degree wheel with pointer at TDC
  • Dial indicator reading at .050" opening and closing

Time Estimate

  • ~90 min. (incl. TDC finding)

Determine Pushrod Length (Pushrod-Länge ermitteln)

Tools

  • CNC-TC6878 Checking Pushrod (adjustable)
  • Caliper (Messschieber) to read length

Procedure

  • 1. Rotate cam to base circle (Grundkreis) – lifter in valley
  • 2. Insert checking pushrod
  • 3. Install rocker, polylock to zero lash + 1/2 turn (= preload)
  • 4. Read checking pushrod length
  • 5. Round to nearest 0.025" (0.64 mm) increment
  • 6. Measure min. 2 cylinders, intake and exhaust separately!

Measurement Entry

Release Criteria

  • Intake and exhaust lengths documented
  • Sweep check (next step) confirms the length

Time Estimate

  • ~60 min.
Rocker Sweep Check (felt-tip method / Filzstift-Methode)

Procedure

  • 1. Color valve stem tip completely with felt-tip marker
  • 2. Cycle valve through full lift (rotate cam by hand)
  • 3. Read contact pattern on valve stem:
  • • Narrow band, centered = pushrod length correct
  • • Band offset outward (toward exhaust) = pushrod too long
  • • Band offset inward (toward center) = pushrod too short
  • • Wide band = wrong length – valve guide suffers!

Photo Documentation

  • Sweep pattern on valve stem (document both good and bad patterns)
Piston-to-Valve Clearance (P-V, Knetmasse)

Tools

  • Modeling clay (Knetmasse), ~0.120" / 3 mm thick
  • Checking springs (light springs) – NOT the #8605 valve springs!
  • Razor blade / scalpel for cutting
  • Caliper (Messschieber)

Target Values

P-V Intake min.≥ 0.080" (2.03 mm)
P-V Exhaust min.≥ 0.100" (2.54 mm)

Procedure

  • 1. Place clay ~0.120" (3 mm) thick on piston crown (under valve pockets)
  • 2. Install checking springs (the #8605 springs are too strong for manual rotation!)
  • 3. Place head with mock-up gasket, finger-tight
  • 4. Rotate crankshaft 2 full turns
  • 5. Remove head, carefully peel clay off piston
  • 6. Cut clay at thinnest point, measure with caliper
Insufficient P-V Clearance = valve hits piston! This destroys valves, pistons, and possibly connecting rods. When in doubt, have valve pockets machined deeper.

Photo Documentation

  • Clay before measurement (mark thinnest point)
  • Caliper on clay cross-section

Target Value

Min. Retainer–Seal gap≥ 0.060" (1.52 mm)

Practical Tips

  • Measure at max lift: retainer must not contact valve stem seal

Guide Plates + Final Pushrods – install
Rockers + Preload (1/4–1/2 turn, on base circle!)

Preload Procedure

  • 1. Rotate crank so this valve's lifter sits on base circle (Grundkreis)
  • 2. Thread polylock onto rocker stud by hand
  • 3. Spin pushrod with one hand while tightening polylock
  • 4. Zero lash = pushrod just stops spinning
  • 5. From zero lash: 1/4 to 1/2 turn further = preload
  • 6. Tighten lock nut (polylock holds position)

4a – Before Start (Vor dem Start)
Pre-Oil (Vorölen) until oil at all 16 rockers

Tools

  • Priming tool (Melling PT11 or long hex bit in drill)
  • Drill (slow speed, clockwise rotation!)

Procedure

  • 1. Remove distributor (access to oil pump drive)
  • 2. Insert priming tool, spin with drill
  • 3. Watch oil pressure gauge – must show pressure
  • 4. Rotate crankshaft 4× 90° during priming (supply all bearings)
  • 5. Remove valve covers – oil must appear at all 16 rockers
No oil pressure after 30 sec = STOP! Investigate (shaft not engaged, pump empty, plug missing).

Oil Change – Cut Filter Open (Filter aufschneiden)

Procedure

  • 1. After 30–50 km (20–30 mi) or 1 hour runtime, drain oil warm
  • 2. Cut filter open with flange cutter or band saw
  • 3. Unfold filter paper and hold against light
  • 4. Clean paper = inspection passed
  • 5. Metal particles = find the cause, do not ignore!
