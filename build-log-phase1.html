Short Block on Stand (Short Block auf dem Ständer)
1. Block Visual Inspection (Sichtprüfung)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Lift short block from crate (2 persons, ~155 lbs / 70 kg)
  • Mount on engine stand (adapter for 302/351W pattern)
  • Remove preservation / shipping oil

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Work light / LED inspection lamp
  • Inspection mirror (dental or telescoping)
  • Magnifying glass for hairline cracks (Haarrisse)

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte)

  • Cylinder Bores (Laufflächen): No scoring, no ridge at TDC
  • Sealing Surfaces (Dichtflächen): Deck, intake rails, timing cover face – no nicks or burrs
  • Threaded Holes (Gewindebohrungen): No damaged threads, no debris in blind holes
  • Freeze Plugs (Froststopfen): All tight, no rust
  • Oil Galleries (Ölkanäle): Gallery plugs present and sealed

Photo Documentation (Fotodokumentation)

  • Deck surface both banks
  • ID plate / casting number
  • Any anomalies (casting flaws, scratches)

Time Estimate (Zeitbedarf)

  • ~30 min. incl. unpacking & mounting
2. Chase Threads & Blow Out Blind Holes (Gewinde durchjagen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Tap set: 7/16"-14, 3/8"-16, 5/16"-18 (11.11 mm, 9.53 mm, 7.94 mm)
  • Compressed air gun with extension
  • Safety glasses (mandatory!)
  • T-handle tap wrench

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • Brake cleaner (residual dirt)
  • Clean lint-free rags
WARNING: Blind holes must be oil- and debris-free! Fluid in a blind hole acts hydraulically – the stud can crack the block.

Release Criteria (Freigabe)

  • All holes free of chips and residue
  • Tap runs freely in every hole

Time Estimate

  • ~45 min.
3. Measure Deck Height (Deckhöhe messen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Depth micrometer (Tiefenmikrometer) or depth gauge
  • Straightedge (Haarlineal) + feeler gauge (Fühlerlehre)

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Deck Height (BHE)8.206" (208.43 mm)
Tolerance (Toleranz)± 0.005" (0.13 mm)
Ford 302 Nominal8.206" (208.43 mm)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Measure at least 4 locations per bank (front, rear, left, right)
  • Deck height + piston deck clearance = quench distance (determines compression & detonation margin)
4. Piston Deck Clearance (Kolbenstand) – all 8 cylinders

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Dial indicator (Messuhr) with magnetic base
  • Straightedge (Haarlineal) across bore
  • Crank turning tool or box wrench

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Deck Clearance (Kolbenstand)~0.010" (0.25 mm) below deck
MethodStraightedge + dial indicator

Practical Tips

  • Most critical measurement of the entire build! Piston deck clearance determines compression ratio, quench distance, and head gasket choice.
  • Measure Cyl. 1 + 5 first – the spread reveals block machining precision
  • Rotate crank to exact TDC (highest point), not slightly off
  • Measure each bore at thrust side AND anti-thrust side

Photo Documentation

  • Dial indicator on straightedge over bore (method documentation)
  • Reading at Cyl 1 and Cyl 5
5. Rotate Crankshaft by Hand (Kurbelwelle drehen) – no binding

Criteria

  • Crankshaft turns smoothly through full 720°
  • No sudden resistance or binding
  • No noises (scraping, clicking)

Practical Tips

  • Turn at flywheel end by hand (more leverage)
  • Binding = bearing issue or debris in oil gallery
6. Check Oil Gallery Plugs (Ölkanalstopfen prüfen)

Torque Values (Anzugswerte)

Gallery Plugs8–10 ft-lbs (11–14 Nm)
Sealant (Dichtmittel)Teflon tape or pipe sealant

Practical Tips

  • Located behind distributor bore – easy to miss
  • If loose = no oil pressure at startup
7. Determine Cam Bearing Type (Cam-Lagertyp bestimmen)

Identification & Target Values (Sollwerte)

  • R351: Oil holes on camshaft side (standard for BOSS block)
  • J351: Offset oil holes (rare, older blocks)
Bearing I.D. #1 (front)2.1295"–2.1305" (54.09–54.11 mm)
Bearing I.D. #2–#42.0835"–2.0845" (52.92–52.95 mm)
Bearing I.D. #5 (rear)1.8595"–1.8605" (47.23–47.26 mm)
Cam Journal ø2.0820"–2.0830" (52.88–52.91 mm)
Bearing Clearance (Lagerspiel)0.001"–0.003" (0.025–0.076 mm)

Practical Tips

  • BOSS 302 block has factory-installed R351 bearings
  • Compare cam journal measurement (next step) against bearing I.D. for actual clearance
  • Oil holes MUST align with block galleries after installation!
8. Main Bearing Clearance – Plastigage (Hauptlagerspiel)

Prerequisites (Voraussetzungen)

  • Block cleaned, bearing bores clean, dry & grease-free
  • Bearing shells assigned to positions
  • Crankshaft cleaned

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Plastigage Green PG-1: 0.001"–0.003" (0.025–0.076 mm)
  • Plastigage Red PR-1: 0.002"–0.006" (0.051–0.152 mm)
  • Torque wrench to 100 ft-lbs (135 Nm)
  • Assembly Lube (COMP 153, Lucas 10153, or Red Line)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Lower bearing shells into main caps (tab in notch, NO oil on bearing surface)
  2. Upper shells into block saddles
  3. Crankshaft onto upper shells
  4. Plastigage strip on main journal – across full width, NOT on oil channel
  5. Main cap on, torque to spec. DO NOT ROTATE CRANK!
  6. Remove cap, compare crushed width to scale → bearing clearance
  7. Record value, remove all Plastigage residue
  8. Repeat for all 5 main bearings

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Main Bearing Clearance0.001"–0.003" (0.025–0.076 mm)

Practical Tips

  • If out of spec, use different shell thickness (Std / 0.001" / 0.002" undersize). Never file!
  • Use Green Plastigage first; switch to Red only if clearance exceeds Green range

Photo Documentation

  • Plastigage strip on journal (before cap install)
  • Crushed Plastigage vs. scale (reading)
9. Crankshaft Endplay (Kurbelwellen-Axialspiel)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Dial indicator (Messuhr) with magnetic base
  • Large screwdriver or lever for pushing crank

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • Mount dial indicator axially against crank snout or flywheel flange
  • Push crank fully forward, zero the gauge
  • Push crank fully rearward, read value
  • That reading = crankshaft endplay

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Crank Endplay0.004"–0.008" (0.10–0.20 mm)
Controlled byThrust bearing shell (position 3 on SBF 302)

Practical Tips

  • Too much play = thrust bearing worn or wrong
  • Too little play = crank binds when hot (thermal expansion)
Camshaft & Valvetrain (Nockenwelle & Ventiltrieb – Werkbank)
10. XE274HR Lobe & Journal Visual Inspection (Sichtprüfung)

Inspection Points

  • Lobe surfaces (Nockenflächen): no scoring, no pitting, no heat discoloration
  • Journals (Lagerzapfen): polished surface, no rust
  • Distributor drive gear: teeth undamaged
  • Cam card included? (Intake Centerline for degreeing)

Practical Tips

  • Light rust on journals = shipping damage, remove with fine Scotch-Brite + oil
  • NEVER use abrasives on lobe surfaces!
11. Measure All 5 Cam Journals (Cam-Zapfen messen)

Tools

  • Outside micrometer (Außenmikrometer) 1.5"–2.5" (38–64 mm)
  • Measure at 2 positions per journal (0° and 90°)

Target Values (Sollwerte)

Journal ø #1–#52.0820"–2.0830" (52.88–52.91 mm)
Max. Ovality (Ovalität)≤ 0.001" (0.025 mm)
Bearing Clearance (Lagerspiel)0.001"–0.003" (0.025–0.076 mm)
12. Morel 5327 Lifters – inspect 16 pcs (16 Stück prüfen)

Inspection Points & Specs

  • Count: 16 pcs
  • Rollers spin freely (check each one individually)
  • Link-bar mechanism functions correctly
Body Diameter (Körper-ø)0.875" (22.23 mm)
TypeHydraulic Link-Bar (HLT)

Practical Tips

  • Soak in oil now! Morel HLT lifters need min. 24h in clean engine oil before Phase 2
  • Establish numbering system (1E, 1A, 2E, 2A... = cylinder + intake/exhaust)
  • Bei 302-Blocks müssen die Zylinderköpfe abgenommen werden, um die Lifter einzubauen! (On 302 blocks, cylinder heads must be removed to install lifters!)
13. COMP 1632-16 Rockers – Trunnion, Ratio

Inspection Points

  • 16 pcs, trunnion (pivot pin) with no axial play
  • Roller tip spins freely
  • Ratio 1.6:1 confirmed on packaging
  • 7/16" (11.11 mm) stud mount confirmed (fits AFR #6411 studs)

Practical Tips

  • Ultra Pro Magnum = steel body – significantly stronger than aluminum rockers
  • Fulcrum design – no separate fulcrum bolt required
14. COMP 4600-16 Polylocks – 16 pcs, 7/16" (11.11 mm)

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte)

  • Quantity: 16 pcs (16 Stück) – count and verify
  • Thread: 7/16"-20 fine thread (11.11 mm Feingewinde) – must match AFR #6411 rocker studs
  • Set screw: Allen set screw present and functional on each polylock
  • Thread condition: Clean threads, no damage or cross-threading

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Polylocks replace standard adjusting nuts – they lock the rocker adjustment permanently
  • Set screw tightens against stud threads to prevent rotation – critical for maintaining valve preload
  • Keep 1–2 spares in toolbox for Phase 3 assembly
Cylinder Heads AFR 1399 (Zylinderköpfe – Werkbank)
15. Sealing Surface Flatness (Dichtflächen Planheit)

Tools

  • Precision straightedge (Haarlineal) min. 14" (356 mm)
  • Feeler gauge (Fühlerlehre) 0.001"–0.004" (0.025–0.10 mm)

Target Values

Max. Warpage (Verzug)≤ 0.003" (0.076 mm) over length
MethodStraightedge diagonal + crosswise, 6 positions
16. Chamber Volume (Brennraumvolumen) – Target: 58 cc

Tools

  • Burette (Bürette) 100 ml, 0.1 ml graduation
  • CC plate (Plexiglas with hole)
  • Grease/vaseline for sealing
  • Measuring fluid (isopropanol + food coloring)

Target Values

AFR 1399 Chamber Volume58 cc
Tolerance± 1 cc
Sample SizeMin. 2 chambers per head

Practical Tips

  • Valves must be closed for measurement (springs hold them shut)
  • Seal spark plug hole with old plug
  • Air escapes through the hole in the CC plate
  • Spread > 1 cc between chambers = head needs resurfacing
17. Rocker Studs 7/16" (11.11 mm) #6411 – confirm

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte)

  • Part Number: AFR #6411 screw-in rocker studs (Schraubstehbolzen)
  • Thread (block side): 3/8"-24 (9.53 mm) fine thread
  • Thread (rocker side): 7/16"-20 (11.11 mm) fine thread
  • Quantity: 16 pcs installed in heads – verify all present and tight
  • Height: All studs same height above head surface – visual check across row

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • AFR 1399 heads come with #6411 studs pre-installed – verify they were not removed during shipping
  • Studs must be compatible with COMP 1632-16 rockers (7/16" stud mount confirmed)
  • Screw-in studs are mandatory for high-lift cams – press-in studs pull out under spring pressure
18. Springs #8605 + Titanium Retainers #8600 – confirm

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte)

  • Springs PAC #8605: 16 pcs, dual spring (Doppelfeder), beehive or conventional design
  • Retainers AFR #8600: 16 pcs, titanium (Titan) – significantly lighter than steel
  • Visual check: No cracked or broken springs, retainers undamaged
  • Spring color coding: All springs should be identical (same color / markings)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • AFR 1399 heads ship with springs and retainers pre-installed – confirm correct parts
  • Titanium retainers are ~40% lighter than steel = less valvetrain mass = higher RPM capability
  • Verify spring and retainer combination matches cam specifications (installed height + seat/open pressure)
  • 7° keepers required for titanium retainers – check next step (#22)
19. Installed Spring Height (Federeinbauhöhe) – all 16

Tools

  • Installed-height micrometer or caliper (Messschieber)
  • Spring height gauge (steel plate with hole)

Target Values

AFR Spec Installed HeightPer AFR data sheet ± 0.015" (0.38 mm)
Correction if out of specShims under spring

Practical Tips

  • Measure without spring: install retainer + keepers, measure gap from spring seat to retainer underside
  • Spread > 0.030" (0.76 mm) between valves = rework valve seats or use shims
20. Coil Bind Clearance ≥ 0.060" (1.52 mm) over max. lift

Calculation (Berechnung)

  • Max. Valve Lift (exhaust): 0.565" (14.35 mm) – this IS the valve lift (at-the-valve, not lobe lift)
  • Formula: Installed Height − Max Lift − Coil Bind Height = Clearance
  • Clearance must be ≥ 0.060" (1.52 mm), otherwise spring or retainer failure
  • Coil bind height is on the spring data sheet (PAC/AFR #8605)
21. Open Spring Pressure at 0.565" (14.35 mm) lift

Tools

  • Spring tester (Federprüfstand) or calculate from rate

Practical Tips

  • Excessive open pressure overloads Morel 5327 link-bars
  • Morel recommendation: max. ~400 lbs open for hydraulic roller
22. Valve Keepers (Ventilkeile) 7° – match titanium retainers

Inspection Points (Prüfpunkte)

  • Angle: 7° keepers (Ventilkeile) – must match titanium retainers #8600
  • Quantity: 32 pcs (2 per valve × 16 valves)
  • Groove count: Verify single- or multi-groove matches valve stem groove
  • Condition: No nicks, cracks, or wear on locking surfaces

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • 7° keepers are standard for titanium retainers – 10° keepers will NOT lock properly
  • Mix-up between 7° and 10° = valve drops into cylinder = catastrophic engine failure
  • Keep 2–4 spare keepers in toolbox – tiny parts that easily get lost during assembly
  • AFR ships correct keepers pre-installed – verify they match retainer spec sheet
Pistons & Connecting Rods (Kolben & Pleuel – Werkbank)
23. Rod Bearing Clearance – Plastigage (Pleuellagerspiel)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Plastigage Green PG-1: 0.001"–0.003" (0.025–0.076 mm)
  • Plastigage Red PR-1: 0.002"–0.006" (0.051–0.152 mm)
  • Torque wrench for rod bolts: 35–45 ft-lbs (47–61 Nm)

Prerequisites (Voraussetzungen)

  • Rod bores clean, dry & grease-free
  • Bearing shells assigned to positions
  • Crank rod journals clean

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Insert bearing shell in rod (tab in notch, NO oil on bearing surface)
  2. Insert bearing shell in cap
  3. Place Plastigage strip on rod journal – across full width
  4. Install cap, torque to 35–45 ft-lbs (47–61 Nm). DO NOT ROTATE CRANK!
  5. Remove cap, compare crushed width to scale → bearing clearance
  6. Record value, remove all Plastigage residue. Repeat for all 8 rods

Target Values & Measurements

Rod Bearing Clearance0.001"–0.003" (0.025–0.076 mm)
24. Ring End Gap – Top & 2nd Ring (Kolbenringstoß messen)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Feeler gauge 0.010"–0.035" (0.25–0.89 mm)
  • Piston ring file (if adjustment needed)
  • Ring compressor 4.000" (101.6 mm) bore

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • Place ring into cylinder without piston
  • Push square with inverted piston ~1" (25 mm) below deck
  • Measure gap with feeler gauge
  • Repeat for each cylinder with its assigned ring

Target Values – Top Ring

Top Ring End Gap0.016"–0.022" (0.41–0.56 mm)

Target Values – 2nd Ring

2nd Ring End Gap0.018"–0.028" (0.46–0.71 mm)

Practical Tips

  • Always measure in the actual cylinder – bore diameters vary between cylinders
  • Too small gap = ring breaks at temperature (thermal expansion). Too large gap = increased blowby
  • Ring End Gap Adjustment: File from both ends equally, deburr with 400 grit
  • Ring Clocking: Top ring gap ~1 o’clock, 2nd ring gap ~11 o’clock, oil ring rails & expander offset from each other
Fasteners (Befestigungsmaterial)
25. ARP 154-4003 Head Studs – measure length (Länge messen)

Target Values & Inspection

Long Studs (Trockenbohrung/blind)6.800" (172.72 mm) – qty 4
Short Studs (Wasserbohrung/thru)5.750" (146.05 mm) – qty 6
Tolerance (Längentoleranz)± 0.010" (0.25 mm)
Thread7/16"-14 block side, 7/16"-14 nut side

Procedure

  • Measure both stud types with caliper (Messschieber)
  • Compare against block thread depth + head height
  • Stud must NOT bottom out in blind hole!
  • Nut thread must protrude 2–3 threads beyond nut
Critical: A stud bottoming in a blind hole will hydraulically lock and crack the block when torqued. Always verify thread engagement depth.

Tools

  • Caliper (Messschieber) 0–8" (0–200 mm)
  • ARP spec sheet (included in kit)

Practical Tips

  • BOSS 302 Block: Hat 351W-Hauptlagergewinde, aber 302-Kopfgeometrie. Stehbolzenlänge prüfen! (Has 351W main bearing threads but 302 head geometry. Check stud length!)
26. ARP 254-1001 Cam Bolt – inspect

Specs & Inspection

Thread5/16"-18 × 1.000" (25.4 mm)
MaterialARP 8740 Chromoly
Torque (Phase 3)25–30 ft-lbs (34–41 Nm) with ARP lube
WasherHardened washer included – must be used

Inspection

  • ARP lube sachet included

27. M-6268-A302 Timing Set – inspect

Inspection Points

  • Crank sprocket: Keyway undamaged, teeth sharp (no wear)
  • Cam sprocket: Timing marks (dots) clearly visible, 3 keyway positions (Adv/Std/Ret)
  • Chain (Kette): No stiff links, chain stretch < 1/8" (3.2 mm) over 12 links
  • Thrust plate: Surfaces smooth, thickness uniform
  • Bolts: 2x cam-to-sprocket bolts (replaced by ARP 254-1001 for cam bolt)

Practical Tips

  • Ford M-6268-A302 is a double-roller set – superior to single-roller OEM
  • Verify keyway position before Phase 3: "0" / STD for initial build
  • Timing marks must point toward each other (dot-to-dot) at TDC #1

Block Praxis-Tipps (Migrated from Main Page)

  • Alle Gewindebohrungen mit Tap durchjagen – alte Dichtmasse und Schmutz verfälschen das Drehmoment um bis zu 20% (Chase all threaded holes – old sealant and debris falsify torque by up to 20%)
  • Sacklochbohrungen mit Druckluft trockenblasen – Öl wirkt hydraulisch und kann den Block sprengen (Blow out blind holes with compressed air – oil acts hydraulically and can crack the block)
  • Kurbelwelle von Hand drehen vor dem Schließen: gleichmäßig und ohne Klemmstellen (Rotate crankshaft by hand before closing: smooth and without binding)
