3a – Cleaning & Preparation (Reinigung & Vorbereitung)
1. Clean Sealing Surfaces (Dichtflächen reinigen)

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • Brake cleaner (acetone-based / acetonbasiert)
  • Clean, lint-free cloths (fusselfreie Tücher)
  • Plastic scraper (Kunststoffschaber) – NO wire brush on aluminum! (KEIN Drahtbürste auf Aluminium!)

Release Criteria (Freigabe)

  • All surfaces oil- and grease-free (öl-/fettfrei)
  • No abrasion residue visible (keine Rückstände)
  • Blind holes dry (Sacklochbohrungen trocken) – compressed air!
2. Blow Out Head Bolt Holes (Kopfbohrungen ausblasen)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Block on engine stand, deck surface accessible
  • All head bolt holes visible (10 per side, 20 total)
  • Safety glasses – debris will fly out of holes (Schutzbrille!)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Compressed air with blow gun (Druckluft mit Ausblaspistole)
  • Brake cleaner spray (Bremsenreiniger)
  • Clean lint-free cloths (fusselfreie Tücher)
  • Flashlight for hole inspection
  • Safety glasses (Schutzbrille)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Spray brake cleaner: Shoot brake cleaner into each head bolt hole to dissolve oil, coolant residue, and debris (Bremsenreiniger in jede Kopfbohrung sprühen)
  2. Blow out with air: Insert air nozzle into each hole and blow dry – direct debris AWAY from engine (mit Druckluft ausblasen – Schmutz WEG vom Motor lenken)
  3. Inspect each hole: Use flashlight to look into each hole – must be clean and dry, no liquid pooling at bottom (mit Taschenlampe prüfen – trocken, keine Flüssigkeit am Boden)
  4. Repeat if needed: If any hole shows residue, spray and blow again (bei Rückständen wiederholen)
  5. Wipe deck surface: Clean deck surface around holes with brake cleaner and cloth (Dichtfläche um Bohrungen reinigen)
CRITICAL: Liquid in blind holes causes hydraulic lock during torquing! The bolt cannot compress liquid – this gives false torque readings and can crack the block. (Flüssigkeit in Sacklochbohrungen verursacht hydraulisches Sperren beim Anziehen – falsche Drehmomentwerte und Blockrisse möglich!)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Water-jacket holes (lower positions 6–10) may have coolant residue – extra attention needed
  • Blind holes (upper positions 1–5) trap oil at the bottom – blow thoroughly
  • Run a thread chaser (NOT a tap!) through each hole if threads look dirty or damaged
  • This step takes only 10–15 minutes but prevents catastrophic assembly errors
3. All Parts + Consumables Ready (Teile & Material griffbereit)

Consumables Checklist (Betriebsmittel-Checkliste)

  • ☐ ARP Ultra-Torque lubricant (included in stud kit / im Stehbolzen-Kit enthalten)
  • ☐ Assembly lube (COMP 159 or equivalent / Montagepaste)
  • ☐ RTV silicone (Permatex Ultra Black or Grey)
  • ☐ Clean engine oil (for lubrication / sauberes Motoröl)
  • ☐ Anti-seize (for spark plugs / für Zündkerzen)
  • ☐ Thread sealant / Teflon tape (for water-jacket bolts / Gewindedichtmittel)
  • ☐ Brake cleaner + clean lint-free cloths (Bremsenreiniger + Tücher)
3b – Cam, Lifters & Oil Pump (Cam, Lifter & Ölpumpe)
4. Camshaft: assembly lube on ALL lobes + journals (Montagepaste auf ALLE Nocken + Lagerzapfen)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • COMP Cams XE274HR camshaft out of packaging, inspected for damage
  • COMP Cams assembly lube #106 (included with cam or purchased separately)
  • Clean work surface – cam must stay clean (saubere Arbeitsfläche)

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • COMP Cams cam lube #106 – generous amount (großzügig auftragen)
  • Clean engine oil for journals as backup lubricant
  • Latex gloves to keep contaminants off cam surfaces

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. All 16 lobes: Apply thick coat of COMP #106 lube to every cam lobe – intake AND exhaust, both flanks and nose (dicke Schicht auf alle 16 Nocken – Einlass UND Auslass)
  2. All 5 journals: Apply lube to all cam bearing journals (alle 5 Lagerzapfen einschmieren)
  3. Distributor gear: Apply lube to the distributor drive gear at rear of cam (Verteiler-Antriebszahnrad am Nockenende)
  4. Fuel pump eccentric: Lube the fuel pump eccentric lobe if applicable (Benzinpumpen-Exzenter)
  5. Verify coverage: Visually confirm every surface that contacts another part is covered with lube – no bare metal visible (alle Kontaktflächen bedeckt)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Hydraulic roller cams are less critical than flat tappet for break-in, but the distributor gear and journals still need protection during initial startup
  • Do NOT use motor oil alone – it drains off during assembly time. #106 stays in place (Motoröl allein läuft ab – #106 bleibt haften)
  • More lube is better than less – excess will dissolve into engine oil harmlessly (überschüssige Paste löst sich im Motoröl auf)
  • Work quickly after lubing – install cam promptly to minimize exposure to air/dust
5. Install Cam (long bolt as handle / langer Bolzen als Griff)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Cam bearings verified in correct position (oil holes aligned)
  • All cam lobes and journals lubed with COMP #106 (previous step)
  • Block tilted slightly rearward if possible (gravity assists)
  • 5/16"-18 × 6" long bolt as installation handle

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Long bolt 5/16"-18 × 6" (threaded into cam nose) as guide handle (langer Bolzen als Führungsgriff)
  • Additional COMP #106 lube for touch-up
  • Clean gloves (saubere Handschuhe)
  • Flashlight to watch bearing alignment during insertion

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Thread guide bolt: Thread the 5/16"-18 × 6" bolt into the cam nose – this is your handle for controlled insertion (Bolzen als Griff in Nockenwellennase schrauben)
  2. Support cam weight: Hold cam level by the guide bolt with one hand and support the rear of the cam with the other (Nocke waagerecht halten, Gewicht stützen)
  3. Insert slowly: Carefully slide cam into block, rotating very slowly as needed to clear each bearing journal (langsam einschieben, leicht drehen)
  4. Watch the lobes: NEVER let lobe edges scrape against cam bearing surfaces – this destroys both the lobe and bearing (Nocken dürfen NIEMALS an Lagerschalen kratzen)
  5. Feel for resistance: If resistance is felt, STOP – pull back slightly, re-align, and try again. Do not force! (bei Widerstand STOPP – zurückziehen und neu ausrichten)
  6. Seat fully: Cam is fully seated when thrust plate surface is reached (Nocke sitzt, wenn Druckplatte erreicht ist)
  7. Remove guide bolt: Unthread the guide bolt from cam nose (Führungsbolzen herausdrehen)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • The long bolt as handle is essential – without it, the cam slides in uncontrolled and can damage bearings (ohne Griff gleitet die Nocke unkontrolliert)
  • Tilting the block rearward (rear lower) lets gravity help guide the cam in
  • Ford 302 has 5 cam bearings – each one is a potential snag point for lobe edges
  • If you feel a lobe catch on a bearing edge, the cam is not centered – never force it through
  • Estimated time: ~15 min. with care
6. Timing Set + ARP 254-1001 Cam Bolt (Steuerkettensatz + Nockenwellenschraube)

Torque Values (Anzugswerte)

ARP 254-1001 Cam Bolt25–30 ft-lbs (34–41 Nm)
LubricantARP Ultra-Torque
Thrust Plate Bolts (Druckplatte)9–12 ft-lbs (12–16 Nm)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Timing marks must point toward each other (dot-to-dot) at TDC #1 (Steuermarken Punkt-zu-Punkt bei OT #1)
  • Cam sprocket on cam, crank sprocket on crank – route chain over both (Kette über beide Zahnräder führen)
  • Use ARP hardened washer (included) under cam bolt (gehärtete Scheibe verwenden)
7. Install All 16 Lifters (alle 16 Lifter mit Montagepaste einsetzen)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • All 16 COMP hydraulic roller lifters inspected (no damage, rollers spin freely)
  • Camshaft installed and timing set in place
  • Lifter bores clean and free of debris
  • Link bar pairs identified (lifters must be installed in matched pairs)

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • COMP Cams assembly lube #106 – for lifter bodies and bores
  • Clean engine oil as secondary lubricant
  • Clean lint-free cloths

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Clean all bores: Wipe each of the 16 lifter bores with clean cloth – remove any preservative or debris (alle 16 Bohrungen reinigen)
  2. Lube bores: Apply assembly lube inside each lifter bore (Montagepaste in jede Bohrung)
  3. Lube lifter bodies: Coat each lifter body with assembly lube, especially the bottom (roller) and sides (Lifter-Körper einschmieren)
  4. Align rollers: Orient each lifter so the roller wheel is aligned parallel with the cam lobe it will ride on (Rolle parallel zur Nocke ausrichten)
  5. Install in pairs: Link bar lifters must be installed as matched pairs – the link bar keeps both lifters (intake + exhaust for one cylinder) aligned and prevents rotation (Paarweise mit Verbindungsbügel einsetzen)
  6. Check free movement: Each lifter must slide freely up and down in its bore with no binding (jeder Lifter muss frei gleiten)
  7. Verify roller alignment: Visually confirm all 16 rollers are sitting on their respective cam lobes and tracking straight (Rollenausrichtung visuell prüfen)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Label or note lifter positions – if removed later, reinstall in same bore (Positionen markieren)
  • Link bars prevent rotation – a rotated roller lifter will be destroyed by the cam lobe within seconds of engine start
  • Lifter-to-bore clearance: 0.0010"–0.0025" (0.025–0.064 mm) – too tight = binding, too loose = oil leak and noise
  • If any lifter does not slide freely: check for burr in bore, wrong lifter diameter, or debris
  • Install all lifters BEFORE the oil pump – easier access to valley area
8. Oil Pump M-6600-D2 – install (Ölpumpe montieren)

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • Fill pump COMPLETELY with assembly lube BEFORE installation! (Pumpe VOR Montage KOMPLETT mit Montagepaste füllen!)

Torque Value (Anzugswert)

Oil Pump to Block (Ölpumpe an Block)22–25 ft-lbs (30–34 Nm)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Pre-fill pump completely with assembly lube (Pumpe komplett mit Montagepaste befüllen)
  • 2. Set pump on mounting pad (Pumpe auf Montageflansch setzen)
  • 3. Insert intermediate shaft – must engage cam gear (Zwischenwelle einsetzen – muss in Cam-Antriebszahnrad greifen)
  • 4. Torque mounting bolt (Befestigungsschraube anziehen)
  • 5. Check rotation by hand – shaft must turn freely (Drehung prüfen – Welle muss frei drehen)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Ölpumpe vor Montage mit Assembly Lube füllen – die Pumpe braucht Füllung zum Ansaugen (dry pump cannot build pressure / trockene Pumpe baut keinen Druck auf!)
  • Verify alignment – pump tends to rotate during installation (Ausrichtung prüfen – Pumpe dreht sich beim Einbau)
9. Oil Pump Drive Shaft M-6605-B302 – install (Ölpumpen-Antriebswelle)

Prerequisites (Voraussetzungen)

  • Camshaft installed with timing set (Nockenwelle mit Steuertrieb montiert)
  • Oil pump mounted on rear main cap
  • Ford M-6605-B302 or ARP drive shaft ready

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • Assembly lube on shaft hex ends (Montagepaste auf Sechskantenden)
  • Engine oil on shaft body (Motoröl auf Schaftkörper)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • 1. Measure shaft length – must span from cam drive gear to oil pump rotor without bottoming out (Länge prüfen – darf nicht auf Block stoppen)
  • 2. Lube hex ends with assembly lube, shaft body with engine oil
  • 3. Insert shaft through distributor bore into block
  • 4. Lower end (hex) must engage oil pump rotor – may need to rotate shaft slightly for engagement
  • 5. Upper end (hex) will later engage distributor gear – verify shaft protrudes correctly above block surface
  • 6. Spin shaft by hand (screwdriver in hex) – must rotate freely and smoothly

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • ARP shaft is one-piece hardened steel – stronger than stock stamped shaft
  • If shaft won’t drop in: cam gear and pump may be misaligned – rotate crank slightly
  • Shaft should have ~0.010"–0.030" (0.25–0.76 mm) axial play when seated – must NOT be tight
  • This shaft also drives the distributor – alignment is critical for oil pressure AND ignition
10. Pickup – install and BOLT (verschrauben, nicht nur stecken!)

Pickup-to-Pan Clearance (Abstand Pickup–Wannenboden)

Target Clearance (Sollabstand)1/4"–3/8" (6.4–9.5 mm)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Too far from pan bottom = air ingestion under acceleration (zu weit = Luft ansaugen bei Beschleunigung)
  • Too close to pan bottom = cavitation (zu nah = Kavitation)
  • Pickup verschrauben, nicht nur stecken! Unter Vibration kann er sich lösen. (Bolt the pickup – do not just press-fit! Vibration can loosen it.)
3c – Cylinder Heads & Valvetrain (Zylinderköpfe & Ventiltrieb)
11. Head Gasket CP331 – FRONT marking forward! (Kopfdichtung – FRONT-Markierung nach vorne!)
⚠ HEAD GASKET HAS "FRONT" PRINTED ON IT! Installed backwards = cooling of one bank blocked = engine damage. Most common SBF mistake! (Rückwärts montiert = Kühlung einer Bankseite unterbrochen = Motorschaden. Häufigster SBF-Fehler!)

Gasket Data M-6051-CP331 (Dichtungsdaten)

Type (Typ)Copper ring, single-use (Kupferring, Einweg)
Compressed Thickness (Pressdicke)0.040" (1.02 mm)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  • Lay gasket dry (no sealant! / ohne Dichtmittel!)
  • Correct side up: "TOP" or "FRONT" must face upward/forward (richtige Seite oben: "TOP" oder "FRONT")
  • Oil/water channels must align with block and head (Wasser-/Ölkanäle müssen fluchten)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Head Gasket hat "FRONT" aufgedruckt – rückwärts montiert = Kühlung unterbrochen
  • Keine Stahldichtung auf Alu-Köpfen final! Die CP331 ist für Alu ausgelegt. (No steel gasket on aluminum heads! The CP331 is designed for aluminum.)

Photo Documentation (Fotodokumentation)

  • Photograph "FRONT" marking visible (proof of correct installation / Nachweis korrekte Einbaulage)
12. ARP 154-4003 Head Studs – hand-tight into block (Stehbolzen handfest eindrehen)

Stud Installation Info (Einbau-Hinweise)

  • BOSS-Block + AFR #1399 use 1/2" (12.7 mm) head bolts (verwenden 1/2"-Kopfschrauben)
  • Lower bolts (position 6–10) go into water jacket (gehen in den Wassermantel) → use ARP Thread Sealant or Loctite 567 on threads!
  • Upper bolts (position 1–5) go into blind holes (gehen in Sacklochbohrungen) + washers with ARP Moly Lube on threads + under nut face
⚠ Water-jacket bolts WITHOUT sealant = coolant leak into oil! (Wasserbohrungen OHNE Dichtmittel = Kühlmittel im Öl!)
13. ARP Ultra-Torque on threads + nut face (Gewinde + Mutterauflage)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • ARP 154-4003 head studs installed hand-tight in block
  • ARP Ultra-Torque lubricant packet (included in stud kit)
  • ARP hardened washers (included in kit)
  • 12-point ARP nuts (included in kit)

Consumables (Betriebsmittel)

  • ARP Ultra-Torque lubricant – from ARP stud kit (do NOT substitute!)
  • Clean cloth for wiping excess
  • Small brush or finger for application

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Apply to threads: Coat ALL stud threads (the portion above the block where nut threads on) with ARP Ultra-Torque (Gewinde oberhalb des Blocks einschmieren)
  2. Apply to nut face: Put a dab of Ultra-Torque on the underside (contact face) of each ARP nut AND on the washer faces (Mutterauflagefläche UND Scheiben einschmieren)
  3. Apply to washers: Both sides of each ARP hardened washer get a thin film of Ultra-Torque (beide Seiten der Scheiben)
  4. Verify coverage: Every metal-to-metal contact surface in the clamping stack must be lubricated (alle Kontaktflächen müssen geschmiert sein)
  5. Do NOT wipe off excess: Leave the lubricant in place – it is designed to remain during torquing (Überschuss NICHT abwischen)

Torque Values with ARP Ultra-Torque (Anzugswerte)

Stage 1 (Stufe 1)35 ft-lbs (47 Nm)
Stage 2 (Stufe 2)55 ft-lbs (75 Nm)
Stage 3 Long/Blind (Stufe 3)80 ft-lbs (108 Nm)
Stage 3 Short/Water (Stufe 3)70 ft-lbs (95 Nm)
Do NOT use motor oil, anti-seize, or moly lube instead of ARP Ultra-Torque! Each lubricant has a different friction coefficient – using the wrong one means the specified torque produces the WRONG clamping force. ARP specs are calibrated for Ultra-Torque ONLY. (NUR ARP Ultra-Torque verwenden – anderes Schmiermittel = falsche Klemmkraft!)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • One packet of Ultra-Torque from the ARP kit is usually enough for one engine – use it all
  • If you run out: ARP sells Ultra-Torque separately (ARP 100-9908)
  • Apply lube just before torquing – do not let it sit overnight exposed to dust
  • Re-apply if studs are loosened and re-torqued (during re-torque after first heat cycle)
14. Set Heads on Dowels (Köpfe auf Passhülsen setzen)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Head gaskets (Fel-Pro 1011-2 or M-6051-CP331) ready – verify correct part number
  • Block deck surface clean and dry (previous cleaning step completed)
  • Dowel pins in block verified (2 per side, pressed into block)
  • ARP head studs installed and lubed (previous step completed)
  • AFR 1399 cylinder heads clean, ports free of debris

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Helper recommended – AFR aluminum heads weigh ~25 lbs (11 kg) each
  • ARP 12-point nuts + hardened washers (from stud kit)
  • Flashlight to verify gasket alignment

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Place head gaskets: Set gaskets on dowel pins – check orientation: "UP" or "FRONT" marking must face correct direction (Dichtung auf Passhülsen setzen – Beschriftung prüfen)
  2. Verify gasket alignment: All water passages, oil drain-backs, and bolt holes must align with block (alle Durchlässe müssen fluchten)
  3. Lift head carefully: With a helper, carefully lower the cylinder head straight down onto the gasket and dowel pins (Kopf gerade nach unten auf Passhülsen absenken)
  4. Do NOT slide: Never slide the head sideways on the gasket – this can damage or shift the gasket seal (Kopf NICHT seitlich verschieben – beschädigt die Dichtung)
  5. Align with dowels: The 2 dowel pins per side ensure correct positioning – head should drop into place with minimal guidance (Passhülsen sorgen für korrekte Position)
  6. Hand-start all nuts: Place ARP washers and hand-start all ARP nuts on studs – do NOT torque yet (alle Muttern handfest aufdrehen – noch NICHT anziehen)
  7. Repeat for second head: Install second head gasket and head the same way (für zweiten Kopf wiederholen)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Copper gaskets (CP331) are installed DRY – no sealant! (Kupferdichtung TROCKEN – kein Dichtmittel!)
  • AFR aluminum heads are lighter than cast iron – easier to handle but still use a helper
  • Verify the gasket did not shift after setting the head – peek through bolt holes with flashlight
  • If a dowel pin is missing or damaged, do NOT proceed – head will shift under torque
  • Some builders use guide studs (long bolts without heads) in 2 corner holes to guide the head down – recommended for heavy iron heads

16. Guide Plates + Final Pushrods – install (Führungsplatten + finale Pushrods)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • Cylinder heads torqued to final spec (previous step completed)
  • COMP Cams flat-style guide plates – correct type for AFR 1399 heads
  • Final-length pushrods (intake and exhaust lengths determined in Phase 2)
  • COMP #106 assembly lube for pushrod tips

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • 3/8" socket or wrench for guide plate bolts
  • Assembly lube (COMP #106) for pushrod tips
  • Clean cloth
  • Flashlight for alignment inspection

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Orient guide plates: Install COMP guide plates with flat side UP (for AFR heads) – check COMP documentation for your specific plate/head combination (flache Seite nach OBEN für AFR-Köpfe)
  2. Slide onto studs: Place guide plate over rocker arm studs for each pair of intake/exhaust positions (auf Kipphebel-Stehbolzen schieben)
  3. Lube pushrod tips: Apply COMP #106 assembly lube to both ends of each pushrod – ball end and cup end (beide Enden der Pushrods mit Montagepaste versehen)
  4. Insert pushrods: Drop final-length pushrods through guide plate slots into lifter cups – intake and exhaust lengths may differ! (Pushrods einsetzen – Einlass/Auslass Längen können unterschiedlich sein!)
  5. Verify centering: Each pushrod must be centered in its guide plate slot – no contact with slot edges (Pushrod muss mittig im Schlitz laufen)
  6. Check for interference: Rotate crank slowly and verify pushrods move freely through entire lift cycle without binding on guide plates (Kurbel drehen, auf Bindung prüfen)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Guide plates prevent pushrod side-loading on valve stem tips – critical for valve guide life
  • Double-check intake vs. exhaust pushrod lengths – mixing them up causes incorrect geometry
  • If pushrod contacts guide plate edge at any point in the cam cycle: wrong pushrod length or wrong guide plate orientation
  • Do not fully tighten guide plates until rockers are installed and preload is set
  • Cam lube on pushrod tips is critical – dry pushrod tips will gall rocker cups and lifter seats on first startup
17. Rockers + Preload (1/4–1/2 turn, on base circle! / Kipphebel + Vorspannung, auf Grundkreis!)

Preload Procedure (Vorspannungs-Prozedur) – 7 Steps

  • 1. Rotate crank so this valve’s lifter sits on base circle (Grundkreis) – lobe pointing away from lifter (Nocken zeigt weg vom Lifter)
  • 2. Thread polylock onto rocker stud by hand (Polylock handfest auf Kipphebel-Stehbolzen drehen)
  • 3. Spin pushrod with one hand while tightening polylock with the other (Pushrod drehen während Polylock angezogen wird)
  • 4. Zero lash = pushrod just stops spinning (Nullspiel = Pushrod stoppt gerade)
  • 5. From zero lash: 1/4 to 1/2 turn further = preload (Ab Nullspiel: 1/4 bis 1/2 Umdrehung weiter = Vorspannung)
  • 6. Tighten lock nut – polylock holds position (Kontermutter anziehen – Polylock hält Position)
  • 7. Repeat for all 16 valves (Für alle 16 Ventile wiederholen)

Target Value (Sollwert)

Preload Morel 5327 HLT (Vorspannung)1/4–1/2 turn (Umdrehung)

EO/IC Method (EO/IC-Methode)

  • When exhaust valve opens (EO), set intake preload on same cylinder (Wenn Auslassventil öffnet, Einlass-Vorspannung einstellen)
  • When intake valve closes (IC), set exhaust preload on same cylinder (Wenn Einlassventil schließt, Auslass-Vorspannung einstellen)
⚠ Only set preload on base circle! If the lifter is on the lobe, the measurement is wrong and the valve stays open. (Nur auf Grundkreis einstellen! Auf dem Nocken ist die Messung falsch und das Ventil bleibt offen.)
3d – Sealing (Abdichten): Cover → Balancer → Pan → Water Pump
18. Timing Cover + Gasket + RTV (Steuerkettendeckel + Dichtung + RTV)

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Torque wrench (Drehmomentschlüssel)
  • Timing cover gasket (Steuerkettendeckel-Dichtung)
  • RTV silicone (Permatex Ultra Black)
  • Front crank seal (vorderer Kurbelwellen-Simmerring)
  • Seal installation tool (Simmerring-Einpresswerkzeug)

Prerequisites (Voraussetzungen)

  • Timing chain mounted (Steuerkette montiert)
  • Oil pan NOT yet mounted – cover overlaps pan rail (Ölwanne NOCH NICHT montiert – Deckel überlappt Wannenschiene)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise) – 5 Steps

  • 1. Press front seal into timing cover – flush, lip faces inward toward motor (Simmerring einpressen – bündig, Dichtlippe nach innen/zum Motor)
  • 2. Gasket dry or with thin sealant layer (Dichtung trocken oder mit dünner Dichtmasse)
  • 3. RTV at transitions to oil pan area (RTV an Übergängen zum Ölwannenbereich)
  • 4. Slide cover over crank nose, press evenly (Deckel über Kurbelwellenstumpf schieben, gleichmäßig andrücken)
  • 5. Torque bolts (Schrauben anziehen)

Torque Value (Anzugswert)

Timing Cover Bolts (Deckelschrauben)12–18 ft-lbs (16–24 Nm)

Practical Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

19. Balancer M-6316-D302 (0-oz, press-on tool! / 0-oz, Aufpresswerkzeug!)

23. Distributor – TDC #1 compression stroke (Verteiler – OT #1 Kompressionstakt)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • MSD Pro-Billet or Ready-to-Run distributor ready
  • Oil pump drive shaft installed (engages distributor gear)
  • Intake manifold installed (distributor drops through intake on SBF)
  • Engine at TDC #1 compression stroke – BOTH valves on #1 must be closed

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Breaker bar or socket on crank bolt (to rotate engine to TDC)
  • 1/2" wrench for distributor hold-down clamp
  • Distributor cap + rotor (for marking #1 position)
  • Marker or paint pen

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Find TDC #1 compression: Rotate crank until TDC mark on damper aligns with pointer AND both valves on #1 cylinder are fully closed – this is compression stroke TDC (OT #1 Kompressionstakt – beide Ventile geschlossen)
  2. Verify compression stroke: If either valve is partially open, you are at TDC overlap (exhaust stroke) – rotate crank 360° more to reach compression TDC (falls ein Ventil offen: 360° weiterdrehen)
  3. Mark #1 on housing: With distributor cap off, identify #1 spark plug wire terminal on cap. Mark corresponding position on distributor housing (Position #1 am Gehäuse markieren)
  4. Set rotor direction: Orient rotor to point toward #1 terminal position as you lower distributor into block (Verteilerfinger auf #1 ausrichten)
  5. Insert distributor: Lower distributor into bore – the gear will mesh with cam gear and rotor may rotate slightly as gears engage. Compensate by offsetting rotor one tooth in advance (Zahnrad greift in Nockenrad – Finger dreht sich leicht beim Einsetzen)
  6. Verify gear mesh: Distributor should drop fully into bore – if it stops short, the oil pump drive shaft hex is not aligned. Bump starter briefly or rotate oil pump shaft with screwdriver (falls Verteiler nicht ganz einrastet: Ölpumpenwelle drehen)
  7. Verify rotor position: With distributor fully seated, rotor must point at #1 terminal position (Verteilerfinger muss auf #1 zeigen)
  8. Snug hold-down: Tighten hold-down clamp just enough to hold position – final timing adjustment comes later (Klemmschraube leicht anziehen – Feineinstellung später)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Ford 302 firing order: 1-5-4-2-6-3-7-8, distributor rotates counter-clockwise (gegen Uhrzeigersinn)
  • MSD distributors have no vacuum advance (no canister) – all advance is electronic via MSD 6AL box
  • The distributor gear meshes with the cam gear at a helical angle – rotor rotates ~15° as it drops in. Pre-offset accordingly
  • Mark the intake manifold at the distributor base so you can verify it hasn’t rotated during transport/handling

25. Valve Covers – 4–6 ft-lbs (5–8 Nm), aluminum! (Ventildeckel)

Preparation (Vorbereitung)

  • All 16 rocker arms set with correct preload (previous step)
  • Valve covers + gaskets (cork or rubber) ready
  • PCV valve and breather grommets in correct positions
  • Gasket surfaces on heads clean and dry

Tools (Werkzeuge)

  • Torque wrench (low-range, accurate at 4–6 ft-lbs)
  • 1/4" drive socket for valve cover bolts
  • Valve cover gaskets (cork or rubber, matched to covers)
  • RTV silicone (thin bead at corners if cork gaskets)

Procedure (Vorgehensweise)

  1. Check rocker clearance: Verify valve cover clears all rocker arm components – polylock nuts, adjusting screws (Kipphebel-Freigang prüfen)
  2. Place gaskets: Set gaskets on head gasket rails – use thin bead of RTV at corners if using cork gaskets. Rubber gaskets typically need no RTV (Dichtungen auflegen – bei Kork dünne RTV-Raupe an Ecken)
  3. Set covers: Place valve covers on heads, aligning all bolt holes (Ventildeckel aufsetzen)
  4. Hand-start all bolts: Insert and hand-start ALL bolts before tightening any (alle Schrauben handfest eindrehen)
  5. Torque in pattern: Tighten in alternating pattern to 4–6 ft-lbs (5–8 Nm) – start center, work outward (Kreuzschema von Mitte nach außen)
  6. Verify PCV and breather: Check PCV valve and breather are in correct positions for your ventilation routing (PCV und Entlüftung prüfen)

Pattern (Schema)Alternating, center → outward
Do NOT overtighten! 4–6 ft-lbs is very little torque. Over-torquing crushes the gasket, causes squeeze-out, and guarantees oil leaks. Aluminum covers distort easily. (NICHT überziehen! 4–6 ft-lbs ist sehr wenig – Überziehen zerquetscht die Dichtung und verursacht Leckagen.)

Tips (Praxis-Tipps)

  • Cork gaskets compress over time – re-torque to 4–6 ft-lbs after first heat cycle
  • Rubber gaskets are reusable and provide a better seal long-term than cork
  • If using stamped steel covers with roller rockers: verify clearance with modeling clay first (Phase 2 step)
  • Tall valve covers (fabricated aluminum) may be needed with stud-mount roller rockers – verify in Phase 2 mock-up
  • PCV valve typically goes on one cover, open breather on the other – check your ventilation system routing
